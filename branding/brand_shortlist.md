# Brand name + domain shortlist

> Resolves open question §10 of the strategy doc. Criteria: Italian-feeling, mountain-feeling, app-store-friendly (short, no collisions), `.it` **and** `.com` ideally both free, no trademark clash (especially in AI/weather).

## ⚠️ Verify before committing
Domain availability and trademark status change constantly and must be confirmed at a registrar (e.g. register.it, Namecheap) and on the EUIPO/UIBM trademark databases. The notes below are directional, based on web research, **not** a legal clearance.

## Competitive name landmarks (avoid collisions)
- **ilMeteo / Meteo.it** — taken, the incumbents. Do not go near.
- **Mountain Maps + "MIA"** — a Trentino startup (founded 2021) already shipping an AI mountain guide with route planning. Closest direct competitor. Avoid anything that echoes "MIA" or "Mountain Maps."
- **Cresta** — strong word, but **Cresta.ai** is a well-funded US AI company → trademark risk in the AI category. Recommend avoiding despite the nice mountain meaning (ridge).
- Bergfex, Windy, Mountain-Forecast — foreign incumbents; our edge is Italian-native, so an unmistakably Italian name helps.

---

## Top recommendations

### 1. **Zerotermico** ⭐ (recommended)
- **Meaning**: *freezing level* — the single most-checked number by every alpinist, ski-tourer and climber. Insider shibboleth: speaks fluent mountain.
- **Why it wins**: instantly signals "this app is by mountain people, for mountain people." Memorable, ownable, not generic weather.
- **Watch-outs**: longish (11 chars); it's a common noun so a clean trademark needs a distinctive logo/lockup. Domains likely available given the niche.
- Domain to check: `zerotermico.it`, `zerotermico.com`, `zerotermico.app`.

### 2. **Vetta**
- **Meaning**: *summit*. Short, punchy, aspirational, easy to say and type.
- **Why it works**: clean app-store name, pan-Italian, scales beyond snow (works for summer alpinism, hiking, later cycling climbs).
- **Watch-outs**: common word → possible existing small businesses; `.com` may be taken. Check `vetta.it`, `getvetta.com`, `vetta.app`.

### 3. **Cengia**
- **Meaning**: *ledge / narrow rock shelf*. Deeply technical alpine term — very ownable, almost zero collision risk.
- **Why it works**: distinctive, brandable, unmistakably mountain. Great for trademark.
- **Watch-outs**: less self-explanatory to casual users; lean into it as a deliberate insider brand. Check `cengia.it`, `cengia.app`.

### 4. **Quota**
- **Meaning**: *altitude/elevation*. Core mountain concept, clean and modern.
- **Watch-outs**: "Quota" is heavily used in fintech/SaaS (quotes, quotas) → high collision risk. Likely hard domains. Lower priority.

### 5. **Nivo / Nivolo**
- **Meaning**: from *neve/nivale* (snow). Short, modern, brandable; "Nivo" feels like a tech product.
- **Watch-outs**: snow-only connotation may box us in when we expand to summer alpinism and cycling. "Nivo" is also a JS charting library (minor, different category). Check `nivolo.it`, `nivolo.app`.

---

## Recommendation

Lead with **Zerotermico** as the brand if a distinctive logo can carry the common-noun trademark, with **Cengia** as the safest-to-protect fallback and **Vetta** as the most mass-friendly option.

Tagline stays: **"Meteo per la montagna, fatto bene."**

Suggested next step: pull `.it` / `.com` / `.app` availability for Zerotermico, Vetta, Cengia at a registrar in one sitting, then run a UIBM/EUIPO trademark search before locking.
